Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Bekon Music ("Bekon," "the App," "we") handles information when you use the App. Bekon is built with a simple principle: there is no account, no user profile, and no backend server collecting your personal data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Bekon does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information (name, email, phone number) to use the App. We do not operate a server that stores your listening history, library contents, or identity.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To function, Bekon reads and stores information locally on your device, including:

  • Your Apple Music library metadata (song titles, artists, albums, artwork, playlists) that you grant access to.
  • Listening activity within the app (play counts, skips, timestamps) used only to power in-app features like Favorites Mix and personalized station-building.
  • Your app preferences (e.g., Mood Tile configurations, filter settings).

None of this local data is transmitted to us or to any third party for storage. It stays on your device (and in your device's local backups, if you use iCloud/iTunes backup, which is controlled by Apple, not Bekon).

3. Apple Music / MusicKit

Bekon uses Apple's MusicKit framework to access your Apple Music library and to play music. This requires an active Apple Music subscription. Your interaction with Apple Music content is subject to Apple's own privacy practices, separate from this policy.

4. Subscriptions and Payment

Bekon Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed entirely through Apple's App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive or store your payment information — Apple handles all billing. We only receive confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active, which is used locally on your device to unlock Premium features.

5. Third-Party Services

To generate descriptive genre, mood, and energy metadata for songs in your library (used to power smarter playback and Discovery), Bekon may send limited song metadata (such as song title, artist name, and existing genre tags — never your personal information or full listening history) to OpenAI's API. OpenAI's handling of this data is governed by OpenAI's own privacy policy.

6. Notifications

Bekon may send you local notifications to keep you updated on app activity. These notifications are generated and delivered on-device; no notification content is sent to or generated by an external server on our behalf.

7. Children's Privacy

Bekon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since your data lives locally on your device, deleting the app removes all locally stored library data, preferences, and listening history associated with it. There is no server-side account to delete.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
